Montlake Cut is a Seattle-themed bar and kitchen from the Flavor Hook group — the same team behind Neighborhood Services, Brass Ram, Town Hearth, and National Anthem. We'd had our eye on it for a while. It's small, reservations go quickly, and the only way in is a phone call.

We called on a Thursday and landed bar seats for Friday at 7:15. The hostess was warm from the first word — specific, unhurried, not a script — and that tone held all the way through the night, ending with our leftovers boxed up without us asking.

The cocktail list earns its own attention. The Breaker ($17) is their house martini built on Ketel One vodka with dill and caper vermouth, celery bitters, olive, and lemon. It's sharply dill-forward — more than you'd expect — and that's the point. One practical note: ask for a fresh cold glass halfway through. The Fresno Gimlet ($17) runs on Arette blanco tequila with fresno chile, cilantro, and lime. Bright, lightly spiced, no gimmicks.

The kitchen operates on the same principle as the bar: take a familiar thing and make one better decision about it. The hamachi ($21) is a small portion and the menu doesn't hide that. The quality earns the price — it melted cleanly. The fluke milanese ($38) is the dish to split: the crust held its texture and the sauce did exactly what it needed to without overwhelming the fish.

Two sides worth ordering on their own: the crab fried rice ($31) came loaded with real crab and egg throughout, not scattered through as an afterthought. The bourbon creamed corn ($13) has backbone — the bourbon is there, not decorative. For dessert, the rum cake ($12) arrives as a compact individual round with whipped cream and citrus, the right size to end the night without regret.

What Montlake Cut does better than most is resist the urge to complicate things. The Seattle theming — vintage Mariners photos on the wall, a Kroll Map Company placemat, a Rainier Beer sign — earns its place because it's specific, not decorative. The food follows the same logic.

Our only complaint: they need a second location.

**Rating: 9/10**
